About

This site collects NewsBaba's public work on GitHub. The projects share one aim: software that works in Tamil as well as it does in English, built in the open so others can check, reuse and improve it.

Ideas, bug reports and corrections are welcome as issues on the relevant repository, for example Tamil OCR issues.
